A little transparency

Good to know.

This is a controlled prototype. Retailers, prices, stock and product illustrations are fictional examples. The editorial photography is AI-created with fictional models: it is inspiration, not actual creator content, an endorsement, or a verified match to the products. Outbound sample links lead to reserved example domains; no affiliate tracking or checkout is connected.

Your wardrobe pieces, cropped images, preferences, outfit feedback and saved looks stay in this browser until deleted. Automatic recognition is disabled to avoid API charges. You can mark several garments in a group photo manually. Wardrobe photos are not sent to an AI provider. Outfit recommendations run on this device. Clearing local data also removes outfit feedback.

Posts, your preview profile, follows, likes, reports and saves stay in this browser. Posted local photo dumps persist in browser storage until you delete them. Reference photos in the budget builder remain temporary. Reference photos are held in memory, never uploaded, and disappear when you remove them, refresh or close the page. No analytics service, marketing cookies or account is used. The app uses local imagery. Archivo loads from Google Fonts, which receives normal font resource requests. The standalone file uses a local fallback font.
