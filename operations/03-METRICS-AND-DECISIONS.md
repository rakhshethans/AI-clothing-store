# Metrics and decision rules

The app's Yes/Not quite buttons store local feedback only; no central analytics collection exists.
Use the CSVs manually for consented observations. They contain headers only: no invented testers.

Funnel: channel impression → landing visit → search → displayed results → user-confirmed useful
result → outbound redirect → network-reported pending purchase → approved purchase → cash paid.
Only the prototype's local interactions can be observed today. A redirect is not a sale or proof
of page load. Real affiliates need network-approved tracking and lawful consent configuration.

Definitions: useful top-five = participant accepts >=1 of first 5 options; activated = meaningful
shopping task completed; D14 repeat = a second meaningful task within 14 days, reporting prompted
and unprompted returns separately. Track denominators, no-match, price mismatches, founder minutes,
cost per query and approved revenue, not just visits/signups.

Proposed resource gates (not statistical industry benchmarks):
- 6/10 manual real-task shortlists useful before automating image matching.
- >=70% usefulness on held-out in-scope tasks before widening catalogue.
- At least 6 of first 20 activated users repeat within 14 days as a directional signal.
- Two permitted sources or a viable direct alternative before claiming cross-retailer coverage.
- Creator tools only if creators actually share and publish again.
- Stop/pivot after two materially different unsuccessful niche/workflow tests.

Spend gates: review at £50; at £100 require a real demand experiment and viable rights path; at
£150 require repeat evidence; retain £50 contingency. Total initial envelope £200, not per month.

Affiliate illustration from plan, not a forecast: 2,000 MAU × 3 searches × .25 outbound rate × .025
retailer conversion × £55 AOV × .025 effective commission × .65 survival = £33.515625 approved
expected monthly revenue. At £.0015 variable cost/search, contribution is £24.515625 before £50
fixed allowance, founder pay and tax. Do not pay for acquisition on an assumed LTV.
