# Retailer access pack

First candidates from 16 October research: Awin New Look UK and Oasis UK/IE public profiles advertise
product feeds. These do not establish your approval, accurate variant stock or broad image rights.
Sources: https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/1946 and https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/57413 .
Confirm current terms when applying. Do not quote an assumed commission as an agreed rate.

Prepare: founder contact/legal identity, actual site URL or private demo if accepted, truthful traffic
(currently unvalidated), target audience hypothesis, screenshots, privacy policy and intended usage.
Do not claim fictional sample shops are commercial partners.

Application description draft (replace brackets; not sent):

We are developing a UK fashion discovery prototype that helps users compare individual garments
and assemble outfits within a budget. Checkout remains on the retailer's own site. We are at an
early testing stage, with no established traffic claim. We seek permission to display a limited
catalogue using your approved feed, with clear retailer attribution and programme-issued links.
Our demo currently uses synthetic products. Founder: [name/contact]. Demo: [permitted URL].

Questions to obtain in writing:

- Can this comparison/outfit use case and domain join? What audience/traffic evidence is required?
- Which product, parent and variant IDs, prices, shipping rules, sizes and stock fields are supplied?
- What is the update frequency and how are deletions/out-of-stock products communicated?
- May we display images, hotlink, resize, cache and retain them? For how long and in which regions?
- May we create/retain embeddings? Send images to a paid AI processor for inference? No training intended.
- Are competing retailer alternatives allowed adjacent to your products?
- Which links/sub-IDs, cookie/consent arrangements and creator/subnetwork rules apply?
- What commission basis, returns validation, attribution/deduplication, payout and termination rules apply?

Data audit: 100 rows/source; 30 listing checks/source on two dates. Count missing fields, price
disagreement, UK delivery, variant completeness and bad links. Do not claim size availability when
only product-level stock exists. Proposed gate: two useful permitted sources, >=95% audited prices
correct and useful top-five candidates for >=70% of 30 actual niche tasks.

Fallback: permissioned manual editorial links and owned/licensed images. Do not scrape a retailer
to bypass failed admission or image restrictions. If no realistic route by week 4, pause integration.
